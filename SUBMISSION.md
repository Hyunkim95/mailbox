# Pigeon

## Tagline

Homing in on what matters.

## Description

We live in a noisy world of offers, updates, receipts and renewal emails. Pigeon gives us personal agents to find what matters and handle supported tasks within rules we control.

Each agent has its own address, inbox and service connections. The agent reads original correspondence, searches supporting documents, compares current quotes and prepares a sourced action. The owner controls payment approval, spending limits, expiry and automatic purchase rules.

In the connected demonstration, Shopping compares coffee offers, purchases one USD 6.50 bag through PayPal's sandbox and receives the original invoice. The same reusable abilities prepare a monthly exact-item refill rule; the normal worker places one USD 29.99 order when due. Both receipts are matched against the authorized payments and merchant status. The USD 36.49 total allowance is then exhausted.

Reel retains its original one-month service, receipt and disabled renewal. Channel3 extends discovery beyond the inbox through real retailer offers. Dashboards can be customized and saved per agent.

Agents compose reusable tools rather than follow hardcoded stories. The server separately enforces identity, inbox boundaries, permission checks, financial limits and idempotent execution. Our merchant SDK exposes supported service abilities through MCP.

The reference merchants, products and correspondence are fictional; the connected PayPal sandbox, AgentMail, model and Channel3 API calls are genuine. No real money or goods move. Channel3 links are retailer handoffs, not supported PayPal checkouts. Missing or unread documents are reported honestly.

## Suggested testing steps

1. Sign in with the privately supplied key.
2. Open Shopping's inbox and inspect its original received offers and confirmations.
3. Ask it to read both purchase receipts, match each payment and read the original invoices. The verified total is USD 36.49 and remaining allowance is zero.
4. Inspect the exact-item monthly rule, its single paid child order and its limits.
5. Open Reel and ask for current service status, paid total, renewal state and access end. Its genuine receipt has no original invoice attachment.
6. Search for current headphone retailer offers through Channel3. No purchase is implied.
7. Open Dashboard, preview a customization and save it.

No further charge is required for judging. The shared workspace's financial controls remain in force. Creating more provider inboxes, resetting identities or re-running payments is unnecessary.
